Emergency Response Center

Immediate actions to take if you suspect financial fraud or account compromise.

Bank & Wallet Fraud

  • Call your bank helpline immediately to block your card or hold transactions.
  • Call 3737 (Easypaisa) or 4444 (JazzCash) for mobile wallet issues.
  • Save transaction receipts and reference numbers for official complaints.
View Guide

WhatsApp Account Hijack

  • Re-install WhatsApp on your phone and enter your mobile number.
  • Request the 6-digit verification code via SMS to log out the unauthorized device.
  • Turn on 2-Step Verification in WhatsApp Settings once recovered.
View Guide

Fake Calls & OTP Theft

  • Hang up immediately. Bank staff will never ask for your OTP or PIN.
  • Do not dial secret MMI codes (such as *21*) sent via SMS or phone call.
  • Forward fraudulent phone numbers via SMS to PTA shortcode 9000.

Common Scams in Pakistan

Overview of frequent online fraud methods and how to spot them.

Easypaisa & JazzCash Fake Receipts

Fraudsters present edited payment screenshots or fake SMS alerts claiming money was sent. Always check your actual mobile wallet balance before handing over items.

View Guide

Fake Bank Representative Calls

Callers claiming to be from bank security ask for account numbers, ATM PINs, or SMS codes. Banks never ask for secret PINs over the phone.

View Guide

WhatsApp Verification Code Theft

Scammers ask for a 6-digit code sent to your SMS under the excuse of a wrong transfer or group invite. Never share WhatsApp verification codes.

View Guide

Telegram & Social Media Job Scams

Promises of quick money for liking videos or small tasks, later requiring victims to deposit funds into unknown accounts. Real jobs never demand payments.

View Guide

Fake Bill & App Download Links (.APK)

SMS messages containing links to download fake electricity bill or update files (.apk) that read incoming bank codes. Only download apps from official app stores.

View Guide

AI Voice & Video Cloning

Synthetic audio or video of family members claiming an urgent emergency or money request. Always call your relative directly on their mobile number to confirm.

View Guide
