Emergency Response Center

Follow simple recovery steps to secure your accounts and respond quickly during cyber incidents.

Mobile Wallet Fraud

  • Contact your bank immediately to block suspicious transactions.
  • Call 3737 (Easypaisa) or 4444 (JazzCash) to block wallet access.
  • Use the Incident Builder to prepare your complaint.

WhatsApp Hacked

  • Re-register WhatsApp to regain access using SMS verification.
  • New code will automatically log out the hacker.
  • Warn your contacts immediately about the compromise.

Fake Bank Calls

  • Hang up immediately — banks never ask for OTP or PIN.
  • Never share codes or click unknown links.
  • Report suspicious numbers through official channels.
