Essential Security Tips

High-impact digital habits to protect your smartphones, banking accounts, and private conversations.

Keep Verification Codes Private

Your WhatsApp codes, banking OTPs, and mobile wallet PINs are strictly confidential. Legitimate bank representatives and support staff will never request them.

Verify Sudden Bank Inquiries

Scammers often falsify official caller IDs. If an urgent caller claims your account is locked and asks for card numbers or passwords, hang up and dial the bank directly.

Enable Two-Step Verification

Turn on Two-Step Verification (PIN) on WhatsApp, Google, and your main email. This stops unauthorized logins even if someone manages to duplicate your SIM card.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about SafePK, its independence, and its educational mission.

01 What is SafePK?
SafePK is an independent cybersecurity awareness platform helping internet users in Pakistan recognize online scams and secure their accounts.
02 Is SafePK an independent project?
Yes. SafePK is 100% independent and is not owned, operated, or funded by any government agency, bank, or telecom company.
03 Who founded SafePK?
SafePK was founded by Kamran Liaquat as an independent public cybersecurity awareness initiative.
04 Is SafePK affiliated with any government department or bank?
No. SafePK has no official affiliation with any government department, bank, or institution; official helplines are listed strictly for public awareness.
05 What does SafePK do?
SafePK provides free scam-prevention guides, security checklists, an incident report generator, and a verified directory of official helplines.
