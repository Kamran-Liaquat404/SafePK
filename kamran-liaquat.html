SafePK Initiative

Kamran Liaquat

Founder of SafePK

Kamran Liaquat founded SafePK as an independent cybersecurity awareness initiative dedicated to helping everyday internet users understand online security, recognize scams, and protect their accounts.

The Learning Journey

Kamran Liaquat is 17 years old and has been actively learning about cybersecurity and digital security. Driven by curiosity about how online systems work and how everyday users can stay safe on the internet, he began studying core digital safety principles.

As part of his learning journey, he completed a Junior PKCERT course focused on providing cybersecurity awareness and education for children, alongside an "Introduction to Cyber Security" course. These learning experiences helped him build a clear foundation in cybersecurity awareness and digital safety habits.

After learning and understanding these concepts, he decided to create SafePK as an independent initiative focused on cybersecurity awareness and education.

From Learning to SafePK

After gaining foundational knowledge through cybersecurity learning and courses, Kamran Liaquat wanted to turn that knowledge into something useful for others.

That idea became SafePK — an independent cybersecurity awareness and education project.

The purpose was to make cybersecurity concepts easier to understand and provide useful awareness resources for everyday internet users, helping people recognize common online scams, protect their personal accounts, and navigate the digital world more safely.
