Support an independent cybersecurity awareness and education project dedicated to helping everyday internet users stay safe online.

Why Support SafePK?

SafePK is an independent cybersecurity awareness project built to provide clear, practical, and publicly accessible digital safety guidance. All educational guides, browser-based safety utilities, and directory resources on SafePK are provided free of charge without paywalls or intrusive advertisements.

Sponsorship and voluntary support from individuals, organizations, and community supporters help sustain and expand SafePK’s educational work so more internet users can learn how to recognize scams, secure their accounts, and use the internet responsibly.

What Sponsorship & Support Helps Fund

Contributions directly support the development, maintenance, and growth of SafePK’s educational initiatives:

Cybersecurity Awareness Content

Writing and updating practical guides covering online fraud, phishing, account takeovers, and digital safety habits.

Educational Resources

Developing structured learning materials, checklists, and interactive safety tools for individuals, families, freelancers, and small teams.

Website Infrastructure

Maintaining reliable hosting, domain services, security, and fast performance so resources remain accessible when needed.

Research

Studying emerging scam patterns, deceptive social engineering tactics, and common digital threats affecting everyday users.

Awareness Campaigns

Running public awareness initiatives that help citizens recognize OTP fraud, phishing links, and impersonation scams.

Video Production

Producing clear, easy-to-follow visual explainers and short educational videos on cybersecurity and digital hygiene.

SEO & Content Development

Ensuring accurate safety guides and official reporting information are easy to discover when people search for help online.

Future Cybersecurity Education Initiatives

Expanding community workshops, student learning modules, and accessible digital literacy programs across Pakistan.

Editorial Independence & Transparency

SafePK is an independent cybersecurity awareness project. It is not affiliated with, funded by, or endorsed by the Government of Pakistan, any government agency, bank, financial institution, or telecom operator. Sponsorship or voluntary support helps sustain public educational work and does not influence the accuracy, neutrality, or independence of SafePK’s educational content.
