8. Official Helplines & Institutional Support Channels in Pakistan

When and How to Contact Official Authorities

If you experience unauthorized account access, financial fraud, or persistent online impersonation, prompt reporting through verified official channels is essential. Do not rely on unverified social media pages or search-engine advertisements claiming to offer "instant recovery." Instead, use Pakistan’s designated institutional helplines and official complaint portals:

  • National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA): Call the emergency helpline at 1799 or submit a formal cybercrime complaint online at complaint.nccia.gov.pk.
  • State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) & Commercial Banks: Immediately call the official helpline printed on the back of your debit card to block compromised cards or dispute unauthorized transfers.
  • Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA): Report fraudulent SMS messages, phishing links, or suspicious caller numbers by forwarding details to 9000 or visiting complaint.pta.gov.pk.
  • National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA): Verify SIMs registered against your CNIC via cnic.sims.pk (or SMS to 668) and protect your identity records through official NADRA centers.
