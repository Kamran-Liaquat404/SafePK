About SafePK

An independent initiative focused on cybersecurity awareness and digital safety in Pakistan.

What is SafePK?

SafePK is an independent cybersecurity awareness and education project focused on helping people better understand online security, digital threats, privacy, scams, account security, and responsible use of the internet.

Independence

SafePK is an independent project and is not a government organization, government department, bank, financial institution, telecom company, or official representative of any Pakistani institution.

SafePK does not represent or speak on behalf of the Government of Pakistan, banks, financial institutions, telecom companies, or other organizations.

Founder

SafePK was founded by Kamran Liaquat as an independent initiative dedicated to public cybersecurity awareness and digital safety education.

Mission

SafePK aims to make cybersecurity awareness easier to understand and more accessible for everyday internet users, empowering individuals, families, freelancers, and small organizations to navigate the internet safely.

What SafePK Provides

SafePK provides free, accessible resources designed to help everyday users recognize online threats and protect their digital accounts:

Cybersecurity Awareness & Educational Content

Clear articles and explanations of common digital scams, phishing, and social engineering.

Security Guides & Resources

Practical digital safety checklists and client-side tools for password security and readiness quizzes.

Official Organization Directories

A reference directory of official helplines and contact information for public awareness.

Reporting Guidance & Public Awareness

Step-by-step incident statement drafting and guidance on how to report cyber incidents to official channels.
