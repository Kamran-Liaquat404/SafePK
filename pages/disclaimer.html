1. No Official Status or Affiliation

SafePK is NOT a government department, ministry, or regulatory agency.

  • We hold no official authority, legal standing, or partnership with federal, regional, or international government bodies.
  • We are a completely independent, non-profit public awareness initiative.
  • Humara Pakistan ke kisi bhi sarkari idare se koi rasta ya direct taaluq nahi hai. Hum sirf awareness phailate hain.

2. No Law Enforcement or Crime Investigation Capabilities

SafePK is NOT a law enforcement agency (LEA), police department, or cybercrime investigation wing.

  • No Investigation: SafePK does NOT investigate cybercrimes, frauds, online scams, identity thefts, or threats. We do not arrest suspects or gather judicial evidence.
  • No Recovery Services: SafePK does NOT hack, recover, unlock, or access user accounts (such as hacked Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram, Google, or banking profiles). We do not provide recovery-on-demand services.
  • Official Redirection: All actual cybercrimes, financial frauds, and blackmailing complaints must be submitted directly to the relevant authorities, such as the Federal Investigation Agency (FIA) Cyber Crime Wing (NR3C) or the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA).

3. Information Accuracy & Guidance Limits

SafePK provides awareness, guidance, educational resources, and automated draft generators strictly for helper purposes.

  • While we strive to compile accurate directories and summaries, we make no guarantees, warranties, or assurances regarding the completeness, security, or accuracy of the information.
  • Any draft or guidance provided is a generic tool. You should consult certified legal and cybersecurity professionals before engaging in formal regulatory processes.
