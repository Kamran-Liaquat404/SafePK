Thank you for trusting SafePK as your education portal. This Cyber Assistance Disclaimer outlines the exact boundaries of any guidance, templates, drafts, or informational support provided on our platform or via our contact support channels.

1. General Awareness & Informational Guidance Only

Any assistance, interactive feedback, draft letters (such as our FIA Complaint Drafts), or manual email support replies provided by SafePK are intended strictly as **general awareness guidance**.

  • Our guidance is designed to explain complex cyber regulations in simplified terms for ordinary Pakistani citizens.
  • It does not constitute official legal counseling, professional security forensics, or authorized bureaucratic advice.

2. Contact Official Institutions for Legal Action

SafePK has no legal authority to intervene in scams or digital disputes.

  • Formal Proceedings: For filing formal legal complaints, launching investigations, tracing scammers, or blocking stolen bank transactions, users must contact official institutions, certified banks, and authorized law enforcement departments directly.
  • Official Help desks: Always utilize registered national cybercrime helpdesks (such as FIA Cyber Crime Helpdesk on 9911 or PTA DIRBS support).

3. No Responsibility for Actions Taken

Your digital safety choices are ultimately your own.

  • SafePK and its platform administrators are **not responsible or liable** for any decisions made, actions taken, or consequences arising from the information or guidance provided on our website or support platforms.
  • All information is utilized entirely at the user’s own risk and discretion.
