Cyber Assistance Disclaimer

LAST UPDATED: JULY 15, 2025 | PURPOSE: ASSISTANCE & SUPPORT LIMITATIONS

SafePK is committed to improving cybersecurity awareness by providing educational resources, practical tools, informational guidance, and publicly accessible digital safety content. This Cyber Assistance Disclaimer explains the limitations of any guidance, templates, educational material, or support made available through the SafePK platform.

1. Educational Guidance Only

Any guidance, cybersecurity resources, templates, complaint drafts, educational content, or responses provided by SafePK are intended solely for educational and informational purposes.

  • Our content is designed to help users better understand cybersecurity risks, online scams, digital fraud, privacy protection, and cyber safety practices.
  • Nothing published on SafePK should be interpreted as legal advice, professional cybersecurity consulting, digital forensic analysis, financial advice, or an official government statement.

2. No Investigation or Recovery Services

SafePK is not a law enforcement agency, cybersecurity incident response team, digital forensic service, or account recovery provider.

  • We do not investigate cybercrimes, identify suspects, recover hacked accounts, retrieve stolen funds, unlock devices, or perform technical recovery services.
  • If you are affected by cybercrime, online fraud, identity theft, financial scams, or account compromise, you should immediately contact the appropriate government authorities, your financial institution, or the relevant service provider.

3. Official Authorities

For legal action, investigations, emergency assistance, or official complaint procedures, users should always rely on authorized government departments and relevant organizations.

  • Official reporting procedures should be completed through the appropriate government authorities and recognized institutions.
  • SafePK cannot process complaints, verify evidence, issue legal decisions, or communicate with authorities on behalf of users.

4. User Responsibility

Users remain solely responsible for evaluating information and making their own decisions regarding cybersecurity, privacy, financial matters, and online activities.

  • Before taking any important action, users should verify information through official sources whenever appropriate.
  • SafePK encourages responsible online behavior but cannot guarantee protection against cyber threats or fraudulent activity.

5. Limitation of Liability

All information, tools, educational materials, and guidance provided by SafePK are offered on an "as is" and "as available" basis.

  • SafePK, its contributors, and administrators accept no responsibility for losses, damages, financial harm, or other consequences resulting from reliance on information published on this platform.
  • Use of SafePK and its resources is entirely at the user's own discretion and risk.
