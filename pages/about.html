About SafePK

SafePK is an independent cybersecurity awareness initiative dedicated to promoting digital safety through trusted educational resources, practical security tools, and reliable cybersecurity guidance for individuals, families, businesses, and communities across Pakistan.

OUR MISSION

Building a Safer Digital Pakistan

As Pakistan's digital landscape continues to evolve, cyber threats such as phishing, financial fraud, identity theft, fake investment schemes, social engineering, malware, and privacy violations have become increasingly common. SafePK was created to help people recognize these threats, understand modern cybersecurity risks, and make informed decisions while using digital services.

Our mission is to simplify cybersecurity through clear educational content, practical security tools, scam awareness resources, and trusted guidance that anyone can understand. By encouraging responsible online behavior and improving cyber awareness, we aim to contribute to a safer and more resilient digital environment for everyone.

"Cybersecurity begins with awareness, and awareness begins with education."

SAFEPK INITIATIVE

Vision for the Future

Founded by Kamran Liaquat, SafePK is an independent cybersecurity awareness initiative operating without affiliation to any government agency, financial institution, telecommunications provider, law enforcement authority, or private organization. Our independence allows us to focus on providing objective, educational, and publicly accessible cybersecurity information.

We envision SafePK becoming one of Pakistan's most trusted public cybersecurity awareness platforms by continuously expanding educational content, improving digital safety tools, maintaining an accurate cyber resource directory, and publishing practical guidance that helps users navigate an increasingly connected world with greater confidence.

Looking ahead, we aim to support students, educators, families, professionals, businesses, and community organizations through innovation, collaboration, and long-term public awareness initiatives that strengthen Pakistan's digital resilience and promote a safer online future for all.
