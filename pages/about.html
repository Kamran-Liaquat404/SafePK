About SafePK Platform

SafePK is an independent digital safety initiative dedicated to improving cybersecurity awareness through educational resources, practical safety tools, trusted information, and AI-assisted guidance for individuals, students, families, and organizations.

OUR MISSION

Empowering Digital Security

Pakistan's rapidly evolving digital landscape creates new opportunities as well as new cybersecurity challenges. SafePK was founded to help people better understand online scams, digital fraud, privacy, and cybersecurity through easy-to-understand educational content, practical tools, trusted resources, and AI-assisted guidance. While no platform can completely eliminate cybercrime, our goal is to promote awareness, responsible online behavior, and informed digital decisions.

"Awareness is the strongest defense against digital threats."

SAfEPK INITIATIVE

Cyber Safety Group

SafePK is an independent digital safety initiative founded by Kamran Liaquat. The platform is not affiliated with any government, bank, financial institution, law enforcement agency, or private organization. Our mission is to make cybersecurity knowledge accessible through educational content, practical tools, trusted resources, and AI-assisted guidance designed to help people navigate the digital world more safely.
