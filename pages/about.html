About SafePK

SafePK is an independent digital safety initiative dedicated to strengthening cybersecurity awareness through trusted information, practical security tools, and accessible educational resources for everyone.

OUR MISSION

Building a Safer Digital Pakistan

Pakistan's rapidly growing digital ecosystem has created incredible opportunities, but it has also introduced new cybersecurity risks. SafePK was established to help individuals, students, families, businesses, and organizations stay informed about online scams, digital fraud, privacy, and cyber threats through trusted educational resources, practical security tools, and reliable information.

While no platform can completely eliminate cybercrime, we believe education and awareness are the foundation of digital resilience. Our mission is to promote responsible online behavior, informed decision-making, and a stronger cybersecurity culture across Pakistan.

"Awareness is the first line of defense against cyber threats."

SAFEPK INITIATIVE

Vision for the Future

Founded by Kamran Liaquat, SafePK is an independent digital safety initiative and is not affiliated with any government, bank, financial institution, law enforcement agency, or private organization.

Our vision is to build Pakistan's most trusted cybersecurity awareness platform by continuously expanding educational content, developing practical security tools, publishing trusted digital safety resources, and collaborating with organizations that share our commitment to a safer digital future.

As SafePK grows, we aim to empower millions of users, support businesses and educational institutions, and contribute to a more secure digital ecosystem through innovation, public awareness, and long-term impact.
