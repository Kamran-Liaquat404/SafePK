Cookie Policy

LAST UPDATED: JULY 15, 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how SafePK uses cookies, browser storage, and similar technologies while providing a fast, secure, and privacy-focused experience.

1. Privacy First

SafePK is designed with a privacy-first approach. We do not build advertising profiles, sell personal information, or use tracking technologies for targeted advertising.

2. Cookies

SafePK uses either no cookies or only essential cookies that are required for the proper operation of the platform. These cookies are never used to identify you personally or monitor your browsing across other websites.

  • No advertising cookies.
  • No behavioral tracking cookies.
  • No third-party marketing cookies.
  • No cross-site user profiling.

3. Local Browser Storage

SafePK may use your browser's LocalStorage to remember preferences such as theme selection, interface settings, or temporary application data. This information remains on your own device and is not shared with SafePK servers.

4. Third-Party Services

Some optional external services or embedded content may use their own cookies according to their individual privacy policies. SafePK does not control how those third parties process data.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control, block, or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling certain essential cookies may affect the functionality of some features.

6. Updates to this Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated periodically to reflect platform improvements, legal requirements, or changes in browser technologies. The latest version will always be published on this page.

7. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Cookie Policy or privacy-related matters, please contact the SafePK team through the official Contact page or email us at infosafepk@proton.me.
