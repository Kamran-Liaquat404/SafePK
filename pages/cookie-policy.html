Cookie Policy

Information about cookies and similar technologies used on our website.

This Cookie Policy outlines how SafePK ensures a clean, privacy-first browsing session without tracking or third-party advertising cookies.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small data blocks stored on your computing device by web servers to track navigation sessions or login state across visits.

2. Our Zero tracking Cookies commitment

SafePK **does not use cookies**.

  • We do not load Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or external marketing cookies.
  • Safar-o-shooq k ley hum kisi b shakhs ko track nahi karte.

3. Local Storage Preferences Caching

Instead of cookies, we use HTML5 **LocalStorage** to cache your preferred application styling (whether dark mode or light mode). This remains purely local inside your own sandboxed browser cache.
