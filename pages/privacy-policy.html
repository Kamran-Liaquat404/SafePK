Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: JULY 14, 2026 | PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION

SafePK respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our website collects, uses, and safeguards information when you access our educational resources, cybersecurity tools, and digital safety services.

Summary (Urdu): SafePK aap ki privacy ka ehtram karta hai. Hum sirf zaroori maloomat istemal karte hain aur aap ki zaati information ko bechne ya ghair zaroori tor par share karne ka amal nahi karte.

1. Information We Collect

Most areas of SafePK can be accessed without creating an account or providing personal information.

  • Educational articles and cybersecurity resources can be accessed anonymously.
  • If you voluntarily contact SafePK, we may receive information such as your name, email address, or the details you include in your message.
  • We recommend that you do not send passwords, banking information, OTPs, recovery codes, or other sensitive credentials.

2. Browser-Based Security Tools

Several SafePK tools are designed to process information directly within your web browser.

  • Where applicable, information entered into these tools remains on your device.
  • SafePK does not intentionally store or process this information on our servers unless explicitly stated.
  • Always avoid entering confidential credentials into any online tool.

3. How We Use Information

Any information you voluntarily provide may be used only for purposes such as:

  • Responding to inquiries or support requests.
  • Improving educational content and website functionality.
  • Maintaining the security and integrity of the platform.

4. Credential Safety Notice

Important Security Notice

SafePK will never ask you to disclose confidential credentials.

  • Email or social media passwords.
  • One-Time Passwords (OTP) or verification codes.
  • Bank account details, ATM PINs, card CVV numbers, or mobile wallet credentials.
  • Recovery phrases, authentication codes, or remote access to your device.

Agar koi shakhs SafePK ka naam istemal karte hue aap se passwords, OTPs, banking information, ya account access maange, to us par bharosa na karein aur foran usay report karein.

5. Third-Party Services & External Links

SafePK may provide links to government agencies, banks, educational organizations, or other trusted resources for informational purposes.

  • Each external website operates under its own Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
  • SafePK is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites.
  • Please review the policies of external websites before sharing personal information.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements to SafePK or changes in legal and operational requirements. Any updates will be published on this page together with the latest revision date.
