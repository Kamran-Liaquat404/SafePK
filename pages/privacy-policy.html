Privacy at SafePK

How we handle information and respect your privacy.

At SafePK ("we," "our," or "platform"), we hold your digital privacy in the highest regard. SafePK is a public, non-commercial cyber safety awareness platform dedicated to educating Pakistani citizens about online security, scams, and digital laws. This Privacy Policy details our strict data practices to ensure your trust is completely protected.

Urdu Summary: SafePK aap ki privacy ka mukammal ehtram karta hai. Hum aap ka koi bhi zaati data (personal information) jama (collect) ya sell nahi karte, aur na hi kisi ke sath share karte hain.

1. Complete Local Execution (Browser-Only Protection)

To ensure absolute privacy, all our interactive digital safety tools (such as the Incident Report Generator and Security Checklists) are engineered to run **100% locally on your own browser**.

  • Zero Server Storage: Your entries, analyzed links, passwords, and form drafts are processed client-side. No data is sent to external servers or databases.
  • Secure Sandbox: Your inputs never leave your device. Humara system aap ke browser ke andar hi kaam karta hai taake aap ka sensitive data mehfooz rahe.

2. Strict "No-Collection" Policy

SafePK operates under a strict zero-data-collection mandate:

  • We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information of our visitors.
  • We do not build user tracking profiles, and we do not use commercial tracking cookies.

3. Absolute Safety Guarantees & Credentials Protection

Critical Safety Rule

SafePK will NEVER ask you for sensitive credentials. We will never ask you for, and you must never input on our site:

  • Passwords to your social media, email, or digital accounts.
  • One-Time Passwords (OTP) or mobile verification codes.
  • Credit/debit card numbers, PINs, bank account numbers, or mobile wallet (Easypaisa/JazzCash) credentials.
  • Direct access to your private accounts.

SafePK aap se kabhi bhi aap ka password, OTP code, banking details ya kisi account ka access nahi mangega. Agar koi SafePK ke naam par yeh details mange to woh scammer hai.

4. Use of Contact Information

If you proactively reach out to us via our official email (infosafepk@proton.me):

  • Any contact information (such as your name or email address) provided by you will only be used to communicate with you to address your query or feedback.
  • This information is kept strictly confidential and will never be shared with third parties or used for any marketing purposes.

5. Third-Party Services and External Outlinks

Our platform contains hyperlinks to official regulatory, legal, and educational websites (such as the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA), Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA), State Bank of Pakistan (SBP), etc.) as well as optional support donation portals.

  • These external websites operate under their own independent terms and privacy policies.
  • SafePK does not control third-party services and holds no liability for their data practices.
  • We strongly encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party websites before submitting any personal details to them.
