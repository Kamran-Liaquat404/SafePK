External Links Policy

LAST UPDATED: JUNE 2026 | PURPOSE: OUTBOUND LINK GUIDANCE

This External Links Policy explains how SafePK uses, reviews, and references outbound links to third-party websites and resources.

Clarification (Urdu): SafePK par di gayi kuch links aapko doosri websites par le jaati hain. Hum in websites ke malik nahi hain aur na hi in par hamara koi control hai. Kisi bhi website par apni maloomat share karne se pehle usay tasdeeq kar lein.

1. Provision of Third-Party References for Educational Purposes

SafePK may include hyperlinks to external, third-party websites, including official authorities such as the Federal Investigation Agency (FIA), Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA), banks, news organizations, and educational platforms. These links are provided solely as a public awareness and navigational convenience.

  • Outbound links are intended to help users locate official reporting channels or access publicly available cybersecurity information.
  • SafePK is an independent, non-governmental educational initiative and does not represent or operate any linked third-party website.

2. No Control, Endorsement, or Liability for External Content

SafePK does not own, operate, monitor, or control third-party websites.

  • Content Changes: External websites may change their content, policies, domains, or security practices at any time without notice to SafePK.
  • No Endorsement: The inclusion of a link does not imply SafePK’s endorsement, approval, or verification of the linked site.
  • No Liability: SafePK is not responsible for the accuracy, legality, security, availability, or content of any external website or resource.

3. User Responsibility and Verification

Before interacting with or sharing information on any external website:

  • Verify the Website: Confirm that the URL is correct, uses a secure HTTPS connection, and matches the official domain. For government services in Pakistan, official domains typically end with .gov.pk.
  • Guard Against Impersonation: Be cautious of spoofed, lookalike, or copycat websites that imitate banks, government portals, or service providers to collect personal or financial information.
  • Protect Your Information: Do not enter sensitive credentials or personal data on unfamiliar or unverified websites.
