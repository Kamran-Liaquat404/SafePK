External Links Policy

LAST UPDATED: JUNE 2026 | INTENT: OUTBOUND_SECURITY_DIRECTIONS

This External Links Policy outlines how outlink elements are utilized, audited, and secured on the SafePK platform.

Wazahat (Urdu): SafePK par doosri websites ke links mojood hain. Hum in websites ke malik nahi hain aur na hi in par hamara koi ikhtiyar (control) hai. Kisi bhi website par apna data likhne se pehle usay achi tarah check kar lein.

1. Provision of Third-Party Hyperlinks

SafePK provides hyperlinked references to external, third-party websites (such as the Federal Investigation Agency, Pakistan Telecommunication Authority, national banks, news channels, and educational networks) as a public service convenience.

  • These outbound links connect citizens directly with official authorities to file reports or study cybersecurity legislation.

2. No Control or Liability over Third-Party Content

SafePK has **no control, monitoring authority, or ownership** over third-party websites, their servers, or their operations.

  • Dynamic Changes: Third parties may alter their domain structures, content, privacy models, and redirect paths at any time without notifying us.
  • No Liability: SafePK does not endorse, verify, or assume any liability or responsibility for the accuracy, reliability, security, or legal status of any material hosted on external third-party platforms.

3. User Discretion & Data Verification Advisories

Before sharing any information or entering credentials on an external website:

  • Verify Web URLs: Users must verify that the target URL is legitimate, has a secure HTTPS padlock design, and displays a correct official domain name (e.g. terminating in `.gov.pk` for government pages).
  • Lookalike Scams Warning: Be highly vigilant against copycat or "lookalike" spoofed landing pages designed to simulate official banks or government portals.
