2. Third-Party Website Responsibility

Once you leave the SafePK website, you become subject to the policies and practices of the external website you visit.

  • Third-party websites may change their content, ownership, security practices, privacy policies, or availability at any time without notice.
  • SafePK is not responsible for the accuracy, reliability, legality, security, availability, or content of external websites.
  • Users should carefully review the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and other applicable policies of any external website before interacting with it.

3. Verify Before You Trust

Before submitting personal information, financial details, passwords, or sensitive data on any external website, users should independently verify that the website is legitimate.

  • Always confirm that the website address (URL) is correct and uses a secure HTTPS connection.
  • Be cautious of phishing websites, fake government portals, cloned banking pages, and other impersonation attempts designed to steal personal information.
  • When accessing official public services in Pakistan, verify that the website belongs to the appropriate organization before proceeding.
