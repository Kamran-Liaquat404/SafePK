SafePK Help Center

Need guidance about online scams, phishing, hacked accounts, privacy, or cybersecurity? SafePK provides educational assistance, practical security recommendations, and trusted resources to help you make informed decisions and stay safer online.

Independent Cybersecurity Awareness & Digital Safety Support

Before You Contact SafePK

SafePK provides educational guidance only. We never request passwords, OTP codes, recovery codes, banking PINs, CNIC photos, or other confidential information.

If you are experiencing financial fraud or an active cybercrime, immediately contact your bank and the appropriate government authorities before reaching out to SafePK.

Account Security Guidance

Learn practical ways to improve the security of your email, social media accounts, messaging apps, and online services using recommended security practices.

  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
  • Improve password security
  • Recognize suspicious login activity
  • Follow official account recovery guidance

Cyber Incident Guidance

If you have experienced an online scam or cyber incident, we can help you understand the reporting process and prepare useful information before contacting official authorities.

  • Evidence collection guidance
  • Reporting procedure overview
  • Complaint preparation tips
  • Official reporting resources

Scam Awareness
